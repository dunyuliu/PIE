# Audit — PIE v1.0.5 present-day solver-failure notes — 2026-09-29

Scope: claims in `docs/notes/failure_analysis_2026-09-28.md` (+ `_scripts/`, scratch `runs/`). Read-only; no re-simulation beyond reading existing outputs. The src/ reads, count re-derivation and reading of the recovery/bias outputs were done independently of the note author (Lars Eriksson: code; Priya Nair: counts, own parser over all logs and CSVs; Ingrid Lindqvist: numerics/statistics; Victor Reyes: direct src reads for §4 and reconciliation).

## Executive summary
- The code-path claims (sys.exit, dead `break`, uncaught SuperLU/IndexError, dead error_code) and every published-data count are **confirmed**: exact matches on the full 18,432 + 18,432 runs.
- The mechanisms are mostly right. The main exceptions: "period-2 cycles" is a 9/32 minority, not 22/31, and the notes misdescribe the 10-m getk2 bug. It is an index wrap-around that `np.zeros` does not fix, not just an uninitialised-memory read.
- **The notes' own tables contradict them.** 15/22 recovered is really 13/22. The "+1 radius" determinism exception is really +30 radii, from a 10-m det(J)==0 case, so "10-m det(J)==0 is physical (0/11)" does not hold. "Recovered models are all no-snow" is false: 2 recovered classes carry snow (7%, 35%), and those classes were left out of the bias correction.
- The bias claim (§4, §7) **cannot go to coauthors in its current form**. Each class has n = 1–3 per MOI; the recovery rates are a best-of-7-variants selection; there are no CIs; snow is confounded with CMR2; and the "~2,800 / ~1,300" scope does not reconcile. The direction is plausible, but the magnitude is unsupported.
- "Physical" means "no root found by the variants tried". Nobody ran a continuation, fold detection or chi-scan, so non-existence is unestablished.

## Conflicts
- Lars (code audit) attributes most 10-m singular-LU crashes to the nrs=0 uninitialised read. The notes and their traces (NaN first appears in RK4, `nan_yc>0`, with v[4]<0 before getk2) say it is chi overshoot. The data support the notes. The cause of the fixed-seed flip remains open (4c).
